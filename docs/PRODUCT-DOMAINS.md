# Product Domains

OpenHouse is an end-to-end real-estate and property operations platform, not merely a CRM.

## 1. Marketplace & Personalization

Public journeys:
- Buy and rent discovery.
- Search and personalized **For You** inventory.
- Saved properties and saved searches.
- Explainable recommendations.
- Private home tours.
- Open-house discovery, RSVP, reminders, check-in, and attribution.
- Conversational property guide.
- Agent discovery.

Success: a prospect finds relevant inventory, understands why it fits, and can move into a viewing, enquiry, application, or offer without losing context.

## 2. Seller & Landlord Acquisition

- List-your-property intake.
- Seller lead qualification.
- Property and market review.
- Listing proposal and agreement.
- Listing preparation: photography, video, floor plans, copy, documents.
- Listing launch.
- Seller reporting: views, saves, enquiries, tours, feedback, offers, and recommended next action.
- Landlord onboarding into property management.

Success: an owner understands how the property will be marketed, what demand is doing, and what happens next.

## 3. Leasing & Resident Lifecycle

- Lead and prospect pipeline.
- Viewings.
- Applications and document collection.
- Guarantor/co-signer limited flow.
- Verification and application decision.
- Lease preparation and signing.
- Deposit and move-in requirements.
- Household members, vehicles, keys/cards, and access.
- Renewal.
- Notice and move-out.
- Final inspection, balance, deposit decision, and closure.

Success: no invisible waiting from enquiry through move-out.

## 4. Resident Experience

- Home overview.
- Balance and statement.
- Rent and maintenance charges.
- Payment.
- Late fees.
- Payment receipts.
- Financing/payment-plan offers from approved providers.
- Service requests and progress.
- Visitors.
- Documents.
- Amenities.
- Notices and communication.
- Household and access.
- Renewal and move-out.

Success: residents can understand their home, money, requests, and next actions without repeatedly calling management.

## 5. Property Operations

- Service requests.
- Work-order generation.
- Triage and priority.
- Assignment to internal worker or contractor.
- SLA and escalation.
- Contractor context and access.
- Check-in/check-out.
- Before/after evidence.
- Parts, labour, and cost.
- Resident confirmation.
- Work-order close-out.
- Preventive maintenance.
- Inspections.
- Equipment/asset history.
- Turnover.
- Cleaning.
- Unit readiness.

Success: every issue has an owner, state, history, cost, and clear definition of done.

## 6. Security & Access

- Guard profiles and shifts.
- Expected visitors.
- Visitor check-in/out.
- Expected contractors/workmen.
- Workman check-in/out.
- Live list of people working onsite.
- Unit/job authorization.
- Deliveries.
- Move-in/move-out access.
- Incident logging.
- Emergency information and escalation.

Success: guards know who should be onsite, why they are there, where they are going, and whether they have left.

## 7. Finance & Collections

- Rent ledger.
- Maintenance fees.
- Other resident charges.
- Late fees and ageing.
- Invoices and statements.
- Payment processing.
- Payment-provider reconciliation.
- Cheque collection and clearance workflow.
- Receipts.
- Deposits.
- Contractor/vendor invoices.
- Approval and payment status.
- Property tax obligations, due dates, payment records, and receipts.
- Financing/payment-plan offers for eligible shortfalls.
- Owner financial reporting.

Success: every charge, payment, cheque, invoice, fee, and obligation can be reconciled to a person/property and has a clear status.

### Financing boundary

OpenHouse should initially facilitate financing or payment-plan offers from appropriately approved third-party providers. OpenHouse must not silently become the lender. Provider eligibility, disclosures, rates, fees, total repayment, consent, and regulatory obligations must remain explicit.

## 8. Intelligence & Ownership

- Occupancy and vacancy.
- Leasing funnel.
- Demand and personalization insights.
- Revenue and collections.
- Service performance.
- Contractor performance.
- Equipment repair-vs-replace signals.
- Turnover time.
- Owner decisions and exceptions.

Success: ownership sees material changes and decisions without needing to operate the property.

## Shared platform capabilities

All domains share:
- Identity.
- Roles and permissions.
- Documents.
- Search.
- Notifications.
- Audit trail.
- Domain events.
- Payments.
- Chat/assistant.
- Analytics.
- Files/media.
- Activity history.
